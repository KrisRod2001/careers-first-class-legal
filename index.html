Careers at First Class Legal

Build Your Future in Property Conveyancing

Opportunities to Grow with a Specialized Firm

First Class Legal is seeking talented professionals to join their team in conveyancing and property law. With a focus on residential property transactions, the firm offers roles that allow you to handle files autonomously and contribute to smooth client experiences.

Positions are available across their offices in Victoria.

Current Job Openings

  • Senior Conveyancer

    Requires 3+ years of conveyancing experience. Must be able to run all aspects of residential conveyancing files autonomously. Positions available in all offices.

  • Property Law Solicitor

    Requires completed law degree and PLT, plus 3+ years in conveyancing. Experienced in managing residential files end-to-end.

  • Junior Solicitor

    Requires completed law degree and PLT (or undertaking online), with 1+ year experience in conveyancing.

  • Junior Conveyancer

    Requires 1+ years of experience, with some background in residential conveyancing files. Available in all offices.

How to Apply

Interested candidates should attach their resume and apply directly through the official careers page.

Why Join First Class Legal?

Work with a dedicated team specializing in property conveyancing, handling a high volume of settlements and utilizing modern practices to deliver exceptional service.
